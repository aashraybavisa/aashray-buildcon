# Aashray BuildCon: business planning

The civil and construction venture from Rajkot-Business (github.com/aashraybavisa/Rajkot-Business) lives here, next to the company's website and marketing, so the plan and the brand stay in one repo.

Scope: construction machinery (trading, rental, parts), civil contracting, site work and materials, in and around Rajkot, India.

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| `market-research/` | demand, competitors, customers, pricing in the Rajkot market |
| `machinery/` | equipment list, specs, buy vs rent, used vs new |
| `suppliers/` | dealers, manufacturers, parts and material suppliers |
| `costing/` | setup cost, running cost, margins, break-even |
| `licences-and-registration/` | GST, MSME/Udyam, contractor registration, insurance |
| `site-and-yard/` | yard, workshop or office location in Rajkot |

Order of work: market check → setup cost → machinery → licences → margins → launch plan.

Shared items that cover every venture (overall budget, company registration, land) stay in Rajkot-Business `00-common/`.
